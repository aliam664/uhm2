🚩 BASES & TERRITORY CONTROL

There are 3 Industrial Bases on the docklands: Alpha (Depot), Bravo (Plaza), and Charlie (Quay).
• Stand inside a base zone to capture it. Multiple teammates capture faster.
• Capturing grants +250 PTS instantly and +10 PTS/sec continuously.
• Base Raiding: Infiltrate an enemy-held base core to drain 200 PTS directly to your team!

🔗 OSMOSIS TEAM SYNERGY LINK

Stay in close proximity to living teammates to activate the Osmosis Link.
Linked agents gain an electric synergy beam, +15% Movement Speed, and 25% Damage Reduction!

👑 SUPER RELIC EVENT

At the 50% match mark, the legendary King of Clubs Relic spawns in Central Plaza Bravo. Securing it awards a massive +650 PTS!
